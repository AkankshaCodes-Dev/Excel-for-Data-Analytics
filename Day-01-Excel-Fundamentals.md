## 📖 What I Learned

Microsoft Excel is a spreadsheet application used to organize, calculate, and analyse data.

- **Workbook:** The complete Excel file.
- **Worksheet:** A sheet inside a workbook.
- **Row:** A horizontal line of cells.
- **Column:** A vertical line of cells.
- **Cell:** The intersection of a row and a column.

### Formatting Practised

## 🧠 Key Takeaways
- Excel helps organize data in rows and columns.
- Each cell has a unique reference, such as A1.
- Workbooks can contain multiple worksheets.
- Formatting makes data easier to read and understand.
